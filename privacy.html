Privacy policy

Introduction

Gen X AV is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you visit genxav.com and interact with our services.

Information we collect

When you submit a project intake form or contact us, we may collect the name, company, email, phone, platform, timeline, and description you provide.

How we use it

We use submitted details to respond to your request, discuss potential services, and improve communication related to that request.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared only when necessary to provide requested services or meet legal obligations.

Cookies & third-party links

This site may use cookies to improve your experience. You can disable cookies in your browser settings. We are not responsible for third-party privacy practices.

Consulting requests and service providers

Consulting requests may include your location, the type of advice requested, and optional photos or documents. We store inquiries and consulting records privately in Supabase. Resend emails the consulting team your submitted contact details, situation description, and uploaded files for review, and sends service notifications. If invited to a paid consultation, Stripe handles payment and Calendly handles phone or Zoom booking. Card details are not stored by this website. Integrator inquiries and homeowner consulting requests are categorized separately.

Consulting content and attachments are generally eligible for removal after 90 days without activity under the existing service retention process. Payment and accounting records may be retained as required. Keep private invitation links secure. For consulting privacy requests, contact consulting@genxav.com.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no online transmission or storage method is completely secure.

Your choices & contact

To ask what we hold, request removal, or ask a question about this policy, email matt@genxav.com.
