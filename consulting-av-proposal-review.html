Before you send a request

Is a full proposal review included in the $99 consultation?

No. The initial offer is a 30-minute Zoom conversation if your request is suitable. Detailed document review, research, and follow-up work are custom-quoted separately.

When is it useful to ask for a review?

Before approving equipment purchases, an upgrade scope, or a significant change is a useful time to raise questions. Include your decision timeline in the request; scope and availability need to be agreed.

Yes. Consulting is available nationwide by Zoom. Use the free request form or call (714) 312-6729 for nationwide consulting.
