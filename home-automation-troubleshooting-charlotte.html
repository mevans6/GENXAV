Existing systems

Start with what is happening.

You do not need to diagnose the problem before contacting us. Describe the symptoms, which rooms are affected, and whether anything changed before the issue began.

  • Controls or remotes that no longer do what you expect
  • Audio or video that works intermittently or in only some rooms
  • A system that became unreliable after an equipment or network change
  • An inherited or unfinished system with unclear documentation

A practical assessment

Troubleshoot first. Plan the next step.

Diagnosis & system rescue

Review the reported symptoms and current setup to identify what needs investigation. The next step may involve settings, programming, equipment, or further onsite assessment.

Programming & refinements

Before contacting us

A few details make the review more useful.

  • Your city and whether you need remote or onsite help
  • The system platform and equipment names, if you know them
  • What is not working, when it started, and what changed
  • What has already been tried and what documentation is available

Common questions

Choosing the right kind of help

Does an unreliable system need to be replaced?

Not necessarily. An assessment can help distinguish an existing-system issue from an upgrade decision. Recommendations depend on the equipment, condition, access, and available documentation.

Can you look at a system you did not install?

Share the platform, symptoms, and background through the Project Intake form. We assess suitability and available access before agreeing a scope; support depends on the particular system.

Do I have to be in Charlotte?

No. Remote programming and technical support are available nationwide. Select onsite service is focused on the Charlotte area. Call (704) 247-7074 for Charlotte-area system support or use nationwide consulting when you need independent advice.
