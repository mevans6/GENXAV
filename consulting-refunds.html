Cancellations & refunds

Free requests

Submitting the preliminary intake costs nothing. No consultation, appointment, or additional work is charged by submitting it.

Unpaid reservations

Scheduling reserves a time conditionally. A separate $99 payment link is emailed with a 12-hour deadline. Reminders are sent at 6 and 11 hours if unpaid, and unpaid reservations are canceled after the stated deadline. Expired checkout links cannot be reused. If a payment is processing or a payment arrives around cancellation, contact consulting@genxav.com before paying again; we will review the payment and arrange service or an appropriate refund.

Consultation cancellations

Use your Calendly confirmation to cancel or reschedule. Cancel at least 24 hours before the appointment to request a refund, or reschedule subject to availability. Late cancellations, missed appointments, and technical problems on your side are generally not refundable. If the consultant cancels or cannot attend, you may choose another time or request a full refund. Applicable consumer rights still apply.

If you cannot book

If no available appointment is suitable or scheduling fails after payment, contact us to arrange a suitable time or request a refund before the consultation is delivered.

Advice and additional work

The fee purchases advice and time, not a guaranteed repair. Additional work follows its separately agreed scope and terms. Deleting a request does not cancel a booking or initiate a refund.

Request a refund

Email consulting@genxav.com with your purchase email, date, and reason. Do not send card details. Approved refunds go to the original payment method; processing time depends on the provider and bank.
