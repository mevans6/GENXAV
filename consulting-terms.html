Consulting terms

Free request review

Submitting a request is free. It does not include technical advice, reserve an appointment, or authorize a charge. We review suitability before offering a paid consultation.

Initial consultation

The initial consultation costs $99 for 30 minutes by Zoom, covering one primary issue or decision. After your request is approved, reserve an available time at least 24 hours ahead using your private invitation and the same email address as your request. We email a separate $99 Stripe checkout link. Payment is due within 12 hours of the payment request being prepared; your email states the exact deadline. Unpaid reservations receive reminders at 6 and 11 hours and are canceled after the deadline, normally within the next five-minute processing cycle. A calendar invitation and Zoom link may arrive before payment; they reserve a time conditionally and do not confirm payment. Your consultation is confirmed after payment verification. Rescheduling retains the original payment deadline, and a verified payment carries over. Payments still processing are checked before any cancellation; do not pay twice. A private invitation does not guarantee a particular appointment time. You purchase professional advice and time; diagnosis, repairs, compatibility, and remote resolution are not guaranteed.

Additional work

Research, detailed proposal reviews, ongoing advisory, and onsite work require a separate scope, quote, and your authorization. The initial fee is not automatically credited toward additional work.

Access and documents

Share only information you are authorized to provide. Do not upload passwords or account details. Keep invitation and paid-access links private. An intake submission does not authorize remote access to your devices. Manufacturer names describe experience and do not imply authorization or endorsement.
